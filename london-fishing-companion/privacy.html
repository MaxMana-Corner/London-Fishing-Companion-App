Privacy Policy

Creel · last updated 2 September 2026

Creel is a free fishing log and field guide for the Thames River watershed in London, Ontario. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device unless you explicitly connect your own Google Drive, and there is no server collecting anything about you.

There is no account, no analytics, and no operator-side database. The app runs entirely in your browser. Nobody — including the developer — can see your catches, your spots, or your photos.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — trips, catches, photos, custom spots, species, baits, knots, tips, and your preferences — is stored locally on your own device using your browser's built-in storage. It is not transmitted anywhere as part of normal use. Clearing your browser's site data for this app deletes it.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for a single permission: drive.file. This allows the app to create files and to open only the files it created itself. It does not allow the app to see, read, or modify anything else in your Google Drive.

  • Files are written to a folder named "Creel" in your Drive.
  • Files are created private. The app never makes them shareable by link.
  • Backups contain your log, your custom content, and your catch photos.
  • Your Google sign-in is held in memory only for the current session. It is not written to disk and does not persist after you close the app.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the Data tab. Disconnecting does not delete files already in your Drive — those remain yours to keep or delete in Drive itself.

The app requests no other Google permission. It does not read your email, your contacts, your calendar, or the rest of your Drive.

Information sent to third parties

When you tap to refresh conditions for a fishing spot, the app requests data from two public services, sending only that spot's latitude and longitude, or a gauge station number:

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — weather and forecast.
  • Environment and Climate Change Canada (weather.gc.ca) — river water level and flow.

No personal information, device identifier, or account detail is included in these requests. Both are used only when you have a connection; otherwise the app shows the last reading it saved.

If you separately set up the optional Google Sheets sync, data goes to a Google Apps Script deployment that you create and control, under your own Google account.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
